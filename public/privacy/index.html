Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective: August 27, 2026

Your training data

Set Context Log stores exercise names, weights, units, reps, RPE, context markers, and notes in your browser’s IndexedDB. If IndexedDB is unavailable, the app uses localStorage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. We cannot see it, recover it, or synchronize it.

Exports and deletion

You can export all set data as JSON or CSV without paying. You control exported files. “Erase all local data” in Settings removes the app’s training records and settings from this browser. Uninstalling the PWA may not remove browser storage; use the in-app control or your browser’s site-data settings.

Purchases

If you purchase the one-time archive unlock, checkout is handled by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record. Set Context Log sends the license token to the Sociobot verification endpoint to confirm access. The token and a daily verification result are stored locally. We do not embed a payment processor or receive card details.

Network and analytics

The core app has no account, advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party scripts, or remote fonts. After installation it works offline. A license check is the only product-initiated request beyond loading app files, and only occurs when a license is stored.

Your choices

You may use the free features without a license, export at any time, clear local records, or block network access after the app is installed. For a privacy question, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
