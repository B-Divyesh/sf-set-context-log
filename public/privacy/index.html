Plain-language policy

Privacy

Effective: August 28, 2026

Your lifting records

Set Context Log stores your sets and settings in this browser. It does not send those records to a server.

The demo uses a separate browser database. Resetting or leaving the demo removes its sample changes without touching your real log.

Exports and deletion

You can download every set as JSON or CSV. The erase control removes the app’s records and settings from this browser.

Browser data can be lost when you clear site data or lose your device. Keep a JSON backup if the records matter to you.

Network use

The app has no account, advertising, analytics, cloud sync, remote fonts, or third-party runtime scripts. After the first visit, the installed app can reload offline.

Your choices

You may export or erase your records at any time. For a privacy question, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
